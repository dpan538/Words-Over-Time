## 3. Scroll 02 replacement — FIVE QUALITIES / 70 YEARS

### Research question

How do five exact phrases that qualify “data” unfold into distinct printed-book histories?

### Data contract

- **Terms:** `sufficient data`, `available data`, `raw data`, `missing data`, `sensitive data`.
- **Years:** 1950–2019 inclusive.
- **Marks:** 5 terms × 70 annual observations = **350 persistent circles**.
- **Source:** frozen Google Books English 2019 exact-string captures in viewer batches 05, 06, and 07.
- **Unit:** uses per million bigrams.
- **Size:** circle area is proportional to annual value on one shared five-term domain.
- **Position at start:** editorial grouping by phrase; within each phrase, year is encoded as seven decade rings with ten years per ring.
- **Position at end:** five labelled horizontal lanes; within each lane x follows 1950–2019 and a small deterministic vertical offset separates adjacent years.
- **Missingness:** all 350 retained annual values are finite and non-negative; no missing point is converted to zero.

### Evolution states

1. **Start — qualities gathered around data:** five labelled geometric fields, each holding 70 annual points. Caption: `DATA TAKES ON FIVE QUALITIES.`
2. **Middle — histories unfolding:** the five decade-ring fields open into five moving streams. Caption: `FIVE PHRASES UNFOLD INTO 70-YEAR HISTORIES.`
3. **End — histories made comparable:** five annual lanes directly label phrase and 2019 value. Caption: `EACH QUALITY UNFOLDS THROUGH TIME.`

The start arrangement is intentionally semantic rather than an unlabeled 7 × 10 microcluster matrix: readers can connect every geometric field to a phrase before motion begins.

## V13 addendum — six independent point geometries

The six square cards no longer share one internal template. Their common system is limited to the 20 px page gutter, 2 px radius, card index, minimum 12 px annotation type, one short footer, and a data-blue ink colour. Each card now owns a different data geometry:

1. **MASS / density field:** 100 points. The faded field records the 1940s `amount` share and the dark field records the 2010s share.
