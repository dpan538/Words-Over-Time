Desktop findings and processed tables are not research authority and do not enter the active dependency closure. The desktop page remains read-only.

Independent does not mean contradictory. Desktop copy is used only as a release-time compatibility baseline—not as an input to mobile calculations. Apparent conflict requires a narrower claim, an explicit method boundary, or a blocked mobile finding; it may not be published as proof that desktop is wrong.

## Principal question

How did **artificial** move from a word about art, making, and non-natural construction into overlapping technical, sensory, social, bodily, and cognitive systems—without ever becoming a simple synonym for **fake**?
