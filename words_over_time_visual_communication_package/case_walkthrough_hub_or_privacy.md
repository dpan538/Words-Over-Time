# Case walkthrough: hub, with privacy as optional substitute

## Recommended primary case: hub

`hub` is the best third case if the article needs metaphor, infrastructure, naming, and dependency. It makes the source-boundary argument especially clean because the page already distinguishes Ngram proxy frequency, strengthened evidence, naming examples, and caution claims.

- `src/components/hub/HubChart03NamingMachine.tsx`
- `src/components/hub/HubChart04CentralityRebuilt.tsx`

## Hub visual sequence

1. Semantic frequency field: five layers use proxy frequency and evidence notes to distinguish mechanical, place, and system-center uses.
2. Transfer model: selected routing terms move from transport and logistics into hub-and-spoke, communication, and network extensions.
3. Naming machine: a radial diagram treats `hub` as a reusable X + hub operator.
4. Centrality rebuild: a dependency layer shows hub as a stable naming format rather than a stable object.

## Hub source-boundary analysis

The semantic field is Signal plus Attestation. It makes frequency visible while retaining evidence anchors and caution notes.

The transfer model is Context. It shows how routing terms travel across domains without turning flow width into exact traffic volume.

The naming machine is Variant. It separates institutional, platform, knowledge, resource, technical, and community naming families.

The centrality rebuild is Boundary. It explicitly resists the claim that hub names one stable object. Instead, the attached word does much of the specifying work.

## Hub claim discipline

Use:

> The `hub` page demonstrates how a visual system can keep metaphor, naming productivity, and source-specific visibility apart while showing their interaction.

Avoid:

> The hub charts prove that hub has become more important in culture.

## Privacy source-boundary analysis

`privacy` is useful for showing legal right, data protection, geography, population, consent, surveillance, and AI-sensitive data as overlapping but source-bounded visual layers.

Its risk is scope expansion. A privacy case can pull the article toward legal history, policy studies, and data governance. Use it only if those domains help the Visual Communication argument rather than diluting it.
