# Consent script

Use this script only after the applicable ethics approval, exemption, or waiver pathway has been confirmed.

## Opening

Thank you for considering this study. The study looks at how readers interpret visual materials about word history and semantic change.

You will compare simplified and evidence-grid versions of selected Words Over Time materials. I am interested in how you understand the evidence, not in testing your knowledge of linguistics.

## Consent points

Please confirm that:

- You are 18 years or older.
- You have read or heard the participant information.
- You understand that participation is voluntary.
- You understand that you may stop at any time.
- You understand that the study will use anonymized participant codes rather than your name.
- You agree that anonymized and aggregate responses may be used in research outputs.

## Recording note

Only record audio, video, screen activity, or think-aloud notes if the approved ethics pathway explicitly permits it and the participant gives separate consent.

## Withdrawal note

Explain the withdrawal process exactly as approved by the ethics pathway, including any deadline after which withdrawal may no longer be possible because responses have been anonymized and aggregated.
