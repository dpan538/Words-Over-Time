# Post-study questionnaire

Use 1-7 scales where 1 means strongly disagree or very low, and 7 means strongly agree or very high.

## Ratings

1. I could tell where the evidence came from.
2. I could tell what the page was not allowed to claim.
3. I understood the difference between frequency and attestation.
4. I understood that frequency does not automatically equal cultural importance.
5. I understood that a missing source result does not prove non-existence.
6. I understood that semantic groups are interpretive groupings, not automatic definitions.
7. I found the evidence-grid condition credible.
8. I found the evidence-grid condition difficult to use.
9. I felt confident about what I could infer from the page.
10. I felt confident about what I could not infer from the page.

## Open questions

1. What part of the material made the source boundaries most visible?
2. What confused you most?
3. Which evidence type did you trust most, and why?
4. Which evidence type did you trust least, and why?
5. Did the rights or boundary information change how you interpreted the page?
6. What would make the display clearer without removing important uncertainty?
