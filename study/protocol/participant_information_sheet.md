# Participant information sheet

## Project title

Words Over Time reader study.

## What this study is about

This study examines how readers interpret visual evidence about word history and semantic change. It compares a simplified frequency-first display with a Words Over Time evidence-grid display that separates frequency, attestation, variants, context, claim boundaries, and rights/attribution.

## What participation involves

You will view a small number of word-history materials and answer interpretation questions. You may also be asked to write short comments about what helped or confused you.

The session is expected to take 25-40 minutes.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason.

## Risks and discomforts

The study is low risk. The main possible inconvenience is mild fatigue from reading and comparing visual materials. The first study round avoids sensitive word pages.

## Privacy and data

The study should not collect names or direct identifiers in the analysis dataset. Responses will be stored using participant codes such as `p001`.

Do not include private personal stories, names, contact details, or other identifiable information in written responses.

## Use of results

Aggregate findings may be used in a research paper, presentation, or supplementary research package. Direct quotations may be paraphrased or lightly edited to remove identifying information.
