# Study protocol

## Project

Words Over Time reader study preparation.

Working article title:
Visualising Semantic Change with Source Boundaries: A Reader Study of the Words Over Time Interface.

## Research question

How does the Words Over Time evidence-grid interface help readers distinguish frequency, attestation, context, interpretive uncertainty, claim boundaries, and rights/attribution when reading semantic change?

## Design

Exploratory mixed-method reader study using a within-subject A/B comparison.

Condition A is a baseline/simplified frequency-first page with minimal source-boundary support.

Condition B is a Words Over Time evidence-grid page or static capture showing Signal, Attestation, Variant, Context, Boundary, and Rights.

The study is intended to test interpretation, not preference alone. It asks whether readers can identify evidence types, avoid overclaiming from frequency, and explain semantic change with appropriate source boundaries.

## Participants

Target formal study size: 12-18 adult participants.

Pilot: 3 adult participants.

Suggested participant groups:

- Design or visual communication readers.
- Digital humanities or language-history adjacent readers.
- General educated readers.

Avoid recruiting minors. Avoid recruiting people where the researcher has a direct grading, employment, or supervisory power relationship.

## Materials

Use three first-round word pages:

- `data`
- `artificial`
- `forever`

Do not use `depression` in the first reader-study prototype unless a later ethics review explicitly covers the added interpretive and mental-health context risks.

## Procedure

1. Give the participant the information sheet and consent script.
2. Confirm adult status, voluntary participation, consent, and withdrawal rights.
3. Collect only non-identifying demographics if approved.
4. Present baseline and evidence-grid materials in a counterbalanced order where feasible.
5. Ask 8-10 structured tasks.
6. Ask the post-study questionnaire.
7. Thank the participant and remind them how to withdraw their data if the ethics route allows withdrawal after participation.

Estimated duration: 25-40 minutes.

## Measures

Quantitative measures:

- Evidence identification score.
- Claim-boundary comprehension score.
- Frequency-misinterpretation rate.
- Source-transparency rating.
- Interpretive confidence calibration.
- Perceived credibility rating.
- Difficulty or cognitive-load rating.

Qualitative codes:

- visible_uncertainty
- grid_clarity
- cognitive_load
- source_trust
- evidence_type_confusion
- rights_boundary_awareness
- design_credibility

## Ethics status

Draft status only. Formal data collection must not begin until the relevant UQ ethics approval, exemption, or waiver requirement has been confirmed and documented.

## Data handling

Do not commit names, contact details, raw recordings, consent forms with names, direct identifiers, or sensitive participant information to this repository.

Only anonymized templates, dummy data, aggregate tables, and aggregate figures belong in the repository.
