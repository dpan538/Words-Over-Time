# Ethics and risk notes

## Status

This is a draft support note. It is not an ethics approval.

Formal data collection must not begin until UQ ethics approval, exemption, or waiver requirements have been confirmed and documented.

## Risk level

The proposed study is intended as a low-risk adult reader study. Participants interpret visual materials about word history and answer comprehension, confidence, and source-transparency questions.

The first study round avoids sensitive word pages such as `depression`.

## Human participants

Because the study involves human participants, a submitted article must include the required ethics statement, approval or waiver information, and consent-to-participate statement.

## Recruitment risks

Avoid participants where there is a direct power relationship, including people the researcher teaches, grades, supervises, employs, or otherwise evaluates.

## Data risks

Do not collect direct identifiers in the research CSV files.

Do not commit raw recordings, named consent forms, contact details, private drafts, or identifiable participant notes to the repository.

Use participant codes such as `p001`.

## Content risks

The initial study words are `data`, `artificial`, and `forever`. These are selected to keep the first study round focused on semantic evidence and visual interpretation rather than clinical, personal, or high-sensitivity contexts.

## Mitigations

- Use a participant information sheet.
- Use a consent script.
- Let participants stop at any time.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary demographic detail.
- Anonymize or aggregate responses before repository use.
- Separate private consent records from public research materials.
