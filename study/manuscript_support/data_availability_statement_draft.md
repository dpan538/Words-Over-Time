# Data availability statement draft

The public repository may include study protocols, task materials, scoring templates, analysis scripts, anonymized dummy data, and aggregate outputs.

Formal participant data will not be shared in raw identifiable form. The repository will not include names, contact details, signed consent forms, raw recordings, full transcripts containing identifiers, or private participant notes.

If ethics approval permits sharing, a de-identified task-level dataset may be released with participant codes, condition labels, task scores, non-identifying participant group labels, Likert responses, and qualitative codes. Direct quotations will be paraphrased or screened to remove identifying information before publication.

If the approved ethics pathway does not permit public release of de-identified responses, only aggregate tables and figures will be shared.
